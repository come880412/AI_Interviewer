Computer Vision Engineer, Acme Vision (fictional example company)
Location: Taipei, hybrid

About the role
Acme Vision builds smart cameras that count and track objects on factory floors and in warehouses. All inference runs on the camera itself. You will develop detection and tracking models, take them from prototype to embedded deployment, and keep them accurate across many customer sites.

What you'll do
- Design, train, and evaluate object detection and multi-object tracking models
- Optimize models for edge hardware: quantization, pruning, and TensorRT or ONNX Runtime deployment
- Work on camera geometry: calibration, homography, and stereo depth
- Build data pipelines for collecting, labeling, and validating training data
- Monitor model quality in the field and roll out updates safely

What we're looking for
Required
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field
- Strong Python, plus C++ for production code
- Hands-on experience training and evaluating deep learning vision models
- Solid understanding of camera models and multi-view geometry

Nice to have
- Experience deploying models to embedded or edge devices
- Multi-object tracking experience
- Experience with large-scale data labeling and model monitoring
